What this is

A DEFLOCK NC CIVIC-EDUCATION DEMONSTRATION

OVERWATCH is a parody. It borrows the look and the powers of a real automated-license-plate-reader dashboard and points them the other way — at the officials who approve North Carolina's surveillance programs, the staff who run them, and the agencies that search the data. Flip to Resident Mode and it turns those same powers on ordinary people going about ordinary days.

The point is simple: it's hard to feel what a surveillance system does when you're not the one inside it. This turns the lens around so anyone can see it from the watched side.

  • Every plate, location, timestamp, association and "prediction" above is fabricated.
  • Plates use a synthetic OVR- format — not real North Carolina plates. No real vehicle is tracked.
  • No home addresses appear. Only public roles and public buildings are shown, and only as commentary.
  • The one exception is the Sharing panel — those figures are real, from public-records research, and should be verified before you quote them.

The ask was never about one vendor. It's about consent: surveillance this powerful should be decided by a public vote and written into a binding ordinance — not left to a setting any police chief can change. Concept adapted for NC from DeFlock Apex's OVERWATCH, whose civic-education demonstration inspired this one.
